## 01 / Flagship products and ventures

### Autom8r — websites, AI and the marketing behind them
**Operating business.** Business sites, custom assistants, customer chat, enquiry-to-quote flows, follow-up automations, social strategy and starter content. I've worked on offers, positioning, local prospecting, outreach operations, reusable workflows and customer-facing experiences.

The point isn't “a site with a chatbot.” It's connecting how a business gets noticed to what happens after someone gets in touch. Individual client deliveries have separate statuses.

### KeepSide — memories, family support and accessible AI
**Interactive prototype / building.** A family memory vault, familiar information, voice/text assistant directions, routines and permission-aware experiences for people affected by memory challenges and those supporting them. Work spans user journeys, demonstration interactions, family/carer views, consent and accessibility.

It is not presented as a clinical intervention or clinically validated system.

### JARVIS OS / JARVIS London Office
**Configured operating system + interface experiments.** A personal AI work environment with 17 specialist role workboards, task ownership, records, reviews, context separation and approval boundaries. The visual “office” is a related interface direction. These are coordinated assistant roles, **not 17 autonomous employees**.

### Vaultara
**Building.** Creative intelligence and content operations: trends, competitor monitoring, meme and campaign libraries, strategist chat direction, content analysis, a production pipeline, messaging integrations and analytics ideas. Built around how marketers actually research and make decisions.

### Pokewatchr
**Mobile product development.** Card and collectibles intelligence: catalogue search, pricing references, sales, deals, watchlists, notifications, portfolio tracking, offline discovery and scanning ambitions. React Native/Expo is the documented direction. Not all features are verified released.

### A8 World
**Research / concept.** A digital marketplace where businesses could discover, hire and assess AI agents through identities, virtual offices, work history and reputation. Includes product strategy, feasibility, storytelling and cinematic trailer experiments. Early digital-economy/token ideas were explored and later deprioritised; no live marketplace or token launch is claimed.

### PeptiPulse
**Product work.** Specialist reference and news experience: a searchable library, approval/news monitoring and a content/video layer. Existing deployment and medical accuracy have not been independently audited.

### Agent H
**Private frontend prototype.** React/TypeScript AI-calling control surface covering agent settings, contact selection, call preparation, dashboard screens and a webhook request path. Some actions remain simulations, and the backend/telephony service is separate from the reviewed source.

### TT Events London — automotive events and car-community app
**Product direction, UI concepts and trailer work; paused/production unverified.** A substantial car-meet application concept, not just an events landing page.

Work included discovery and upcoming meets, private/request/invite access, live event updates, scheduled organiser-controlled location drops, convoy journeys, crews and car profiles, voting/winners/leaderboards, announcements, moderation/safety ideas, shop/partners and **TT Radio** interaction concepts. The design evolved through Home/Meets/Live/Chats/Garage and Discover/Live/Community/Shop directions.

I also worked on app experience, design briefs, product specifications and promotional video/trailer concepts. React Native/Expo, Supabase, LiveKit and an admin web interface were explored as a possible stack. This is **not** a claim of a live location-sharing service, production radio network, published app or approved final deployment.
