## 1 / Audience understanding before feature lists

My entry point was influencer marketing and campaign strategy. I know how to research creators, understand a community, shape a content angle and find the simplest version of an offer people understand.

**Where I've applied it:** creator-evaluation workflows, community campaigns, local-business outreach, artist and brand positioning, event promotion and creative-intelligence products.

**What I bring:** audience insight, hooks, campaign concepts, distribution strategy, launch planning and an instinct for whether the problem is worth building for.

## 2 / Product experiences people can use

I've designed and developed working interfaces for AI calling, business services and browser-based operations, plus prototype journeys for automotive events, collectibles intelligence and accessible family support.

**What I bring:** user flows, React/TypeScript frontends, forms and state, mobile product thinking, clear onboarding and the ability to turn a rough requirement into a credible demonstration.

**Where the line is:** not every prototype has a production backend or all its planned features.

## 3 / AI that fits into a workflow

Assistants are useful when they know what they're allowed to do, where the information comes from and when a human needs to step in.

**Where I've applied it:** structured JARVIS role workspaces, website-assistant concepts, an AI-calling control interface, internal business knowledge systems and enquiry-handling workflows.

**What I bring:** orchestration thinking, API/webhook boundaries, permissions, handoffs, review states and practical AI product scoping.

## 4 / Commercial and operational thinking

I don't stop at the website. I care about the chain from discovery to enquiry, response, follow-up and delivery.

**Where I've applied it:** agency operations, lead research and outreach, CRM-like demos, quoting workflows, stock/job records and small-business automation concepts.

**What I bring:** connecting front-of-house UX with the data and processes behind it; being clear about which parts are operational and which are still demos.

## 5 / Creative direction, not just technical implementation

I've worked on social-first brand positioning, character-led campaigns, short-video concepts, launch trailers, motion-driven websites and the marketing story around new products.

**What I bring:** the ability to speak both product and creative, so the people building something and the people presenting it are working toward the same thing.

I keep private clients and collaborators private; project stage and individual contribution matter more than name-dropping.
