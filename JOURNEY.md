# The build journey

**autom8r.haych**

A project-led record across software, AI, automation and product design.

## Marketing technology

Early exploration focused on the connection between content, audiences and business processes. Isearch explored influencer evaluation, while content-automation ideas examined repetitive work around publishing and coordination.

That work shaped a practical approach to products: clarify the problem, understand the workflow and make the result useful.

## Business websites and automation

Autom8r brings together business websites, tailored AI assistants, customer chatbots and workflow automation.

The aim is to connect the way a business presents itself with the way it handles enquiries and everyday tasks.

**Stage:** active business with a public website. Individual systems have their own implementation scope.

[Explore Autom8r](https://autom8r.uk/)

## Voice agents and control panels

Agent H explores how someone configures a calling assistant, prepares a request and reviews activity.

The control-panel prototype uses React, TypeScript, Vite, Tailwind CSS and shadcn/ui. It combines interface screens with a request path to an external backend.

**Stage:** prototype. Demonstration behaviour is documented separately from verified backend behaviour. Source remains private.

The next engineering step is connecting the interface to real authentication, durable storage and accurate call outcomes.

## Web applications and data

Work across Python, PHP and MySQL has explored object-oriented structure, user interfaces and relational data.

CRUD applications provide a useful place to practise how identity, ownership, validation and database operations fit together. The same principles matter when an AI feature becomes part of a real application.

## Accessible product design

CalmCart explored a clearer shopping interface for older users. KeepSide brings attention to another demanding use case: helping families organise familiar people, memories and routines.

**KeepSide stage:** interactive prototype and product in development. The next step is learning what intended users and practitioners find useful. Clinical effectiveness is not established.

[Explore KeepSide](https://keepside.uk/)

## AI education

The AI Founders Initiative explores how people can move from using AI to testing a practical business idea.

The public classroom demonstration works with prepared examples and asks users to check claims, examine costs and inspect a small prototype.

**Stage:** demonstration available; programme in development.

[Explore the classroom demonstration](https://autom8r.uk/ai-founders/lab)
