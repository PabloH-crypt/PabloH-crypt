## 01 / Ventures and products

### Autom8r — the business layer
**Active agency.** Building business websites, tailored AI assistants, customer-facing chat and enquiry/follow-up automations, alongside social media strategy and content direction.

What interests me is the full chain: **someone discovers the business → understands it → makes an enquiry → gets the right follow-up**. The offer joins creative distribution to practical systems. Client outcomes and individual implementations have their own evidence and status.

### KeepSide — human-centred AI
**Interactive prototype; in development.** A family-centred memory and routine-support experience, bringing familiar stories, media, personal information and an assistant together.

My focus includes clear interfaces, family-reviewed information, permissions, carer access and responsible handling of sensitive data. Demonstrations use fictional information. It's **not** presented as a clinically validated treatment or care service.

### Vaultara — creative intelligence
**Product development.** Exploring software that connects trend and competitor research to campaign concepts, creative libraries, content analysis, strategist workflows and publishing operations.

The principle: make good research and stronger creative easier to do — not just generate endless content.

### Pokewatchr — collecting meets data
**Mobile product in development.** Researching catalogue search, watchlists, portfolio tools, price references, deals and scanning in a collectibles-focused experience. Mobile UX and data handling are key parts of the work. Listed capabilities describe the product direction, not a confirmed complete release.

### JARVIS OS — my operating workspace
**Configured workspace; interfaces evolving.** Specialist roles, task ownership, research records, review stages, handoffs and approval boundaries. A way of coordinating product, marketing and operations across multiple areas of work.

The specialist roles are structured ways of working, not a claim of independently operating autonomous employees.

### Agent H — control panel for AI calling
**Private frontend prototype.** React/TypeScript dashboard for agent setup, contact selection, call preparation and configuration. A webhook route can send a request to an external service; several other screens use simulated behaviour. Secure production authentication and verified call completion are separate work.

### A8 World — where AI agents could work
**Research and concept work.** A marketplace vision for agent discovery, identity, reputation and virtual offices. Includes platform thinking, product positioning and cinematic concept/trailer work. No marketplace deployment is claimed.

### PeptiPulse — specialist information product
**Product work; current deployment unverified.** Searchable reference-library and news-tracking direction, with future content and video workflows explored. No healthcare or medical-result claims.

## 03 / Marketing, creative and growth

This isn't an add-on to the technical work. It is where a lot of my product instincts came from.

**Influencer and creator work.** Creator research and fit, outreach, campaign planning, content briefs, social formats, event-related coordination and thinking through the journey from awareness to action.

**Artist and brand direction.** Distinctive portfolio-site ideas, visual presentation, motion graphics direction, creator positioning, short-form POV concepts, launch planning and the practical path from content to enquiries.

**Community and culture-led campaigns.** Character/mascot consistency, meme formats, platform-native copy, short-video concepts and ways to create recognisable content in fast-moving online communities.

**Creative systems.** Trend research, competitor intelligence, content queues, campaign planning, creator evaluation and analytics concepts. Software informed by how creative work actually gets made.

**Commercial automations.** Lead research and qualification, tailored outreach, enquiry capture, CRM-like workflows, quoting and follow-up. Drafts and proposals are not claimed as delivered results or sales.

Some engagements were paid, some were strategic proposals and some were experiments; confidential commercial terms and unverified performance claims stay off this page.

## 04 / Technical foundation

**Interfaces:** React, TypeScript, JavaScript, HTML/CSS, mobile UI concepts, responsive and accessibility-focused design.

**Data and application logic:** Python, PHP, SQL/MySQL, relational models, CRUD, form validation and state.

**Integrations and systems:** HTTP APIs, webhooks, workflow automation, assistant patterns, permissions, source-of-truth design, human review and operational handoffs.

Across the portfolio, the engineering task changes. What stays consistent is thinking beyond the front page: what the user does, what happens behind the scenes and who owns the result.
